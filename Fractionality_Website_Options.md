# Fractionality — Website Options
*Pre-read for call: May 18, 2026*

---

## What we need right now

A simple, professional web presence that all three of us can own and update — without needing a developer every time something changes.

Three options worth considering.

---

## Option 1: Notion

**What it is:** A document tool that can be published as a website with a custom domain.

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Anyone can edit — no technical knowledge needed | Looks like Notion, not like a brand |
| Free to start | Limited design control |
| Also works as an internal wiki/knowledge base | Feels lightweight for a professional platform |
| AI can make edits on instruction | Custom domain needs a paid plan (~$10/month) |

**Best for:** Getting something up in one day. Low effort, low polish.

---

## Option 2: Framer ⭐ Recommended

**What it is:** A modern no-code website builder with AI-assisted editing. Designed for founders who want a real website without a developer.

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Beautiful, professional design out of the box | Small learning curve to set up initially |
| Non-technical people can manage and edit | Paid plan needed for custom domain (~$15/month) |
| AI can make changes on instruction | Less suited for heavy content like a blog |
| Responsive and fast | |
| Lots of startup/platform templates | |

**Best for:** A proper brand presence that all three of us can own and update. The sweet spot between design quality and ease of use.

---

## Option 3: Webflow

**What it is:** The most powerful no-code website builder. Used by professional designers and startups.

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| Full design control | Steep learning curve — not truly non-technical |
| Can handle complex layouts and interactions | Most expensive (~$23/month+) |
| Built-in CMS for blog/content | Changes require technical familiarity |
| Professional grade | Overkill for where we are right now |

**Best for:** When Fractionality scales and needs a fully custom platform. Not for now.

---

## Decision for tonight

| Question | Answer needed |
|---|---|
| Do we want a website or a Notion page? | Website (Framer) vs. Notion |
| Who owns the first version? | One person sets it up, all three can edit |
| What goes on it at launch? | What we are, who we help, how to reach us |

---

## Recommendation

**Start with Framer.** It gives us a real website that looks credible to clients, all three of us can update it without help, and an AI agent can make changes on instruction. Setup takes one weekend session.

Notion is faster but will feel like a placeholder the moment we share it with a potential client.
