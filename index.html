When You Face a Leadership Gap

The difference between short-term fixes and lasting impact.

Wrong Way

Guess, Grind, and Settle

1
You face a problem. Growth is stalling, the team is misaligned, or the numbers aren't adding up. You know something needs to change.
2
You decide whom to hire. Without a clear framework, you guess at the role — CFO? CMO? "Someone senior" — and start searching.
3
Everyone is out of budget. Full-time CXOs cost ₹40L–₹1Cr+. Consultants charge ₹5L+ with no execution ownership. The math doesn't work.
4
You hire a freelancer. You settle for the cheapest option. They deliver short-term outputs, not outcomes. The problem resurfaces in 3 months.
✗

Short-term results. Long-term cost. The "fix" creates downstream problems — misaligned strategy, wasted spend, and a team that's seen it all before.

Right Way

Share, Match, and Lead

1
You share your problem with Fractionalty. No job title needed. Just describe what's breaking, what's urgent, and where leadership is missing.
2
We arrange three interviews. We match you with vetted executives who've solved the same or similar problems — or have the expertise to diagnose and articulate a solution.
3
You choose your fractional executive. After the interviews, you bring the right leader on board — with clear scope, defined expectations, and real ownership.
4
They lead. Your business moves forward. An embedded leader who diagnoses the real problem, drives execution, and strengthens your team — without the full-time cost.
✓

Better outcome. Real leadership. The right person, matched to the right problem, operating with the authority and context to deliver lasting impact.
