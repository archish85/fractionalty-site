# Fractionalty Website Sitemap & Wireframe Copy Draft

## Purpose
This document translates Fractionalty's current positioning into a practical website structure and first-pass copy draft for design and build.

## Core Positioning
**Primary headline**

Embedded Business Leadership, Without the Full-Time Cost

**Core promise**

Fractionalty helps Indian startups and SMBs turn messy business problems into the right embedded CXO hire, with more confidence, less hiring friction, and without the cost or rigidity of a full-time executive.

## Primary Audience
- Founders and CEOs of growing Indian startups and SMBs
- Business leaders who know they have a leadership gap but are not ready for a full-time CXO
- Buyers who want outcomes and ownership, not generic consulting or low-cost talent

## What The Website Must Do
- Make the problem feel instantly familiar
- Establish that Fractionalty is not a freelancer marketplace or consulting brochure
- Explain the matching journey clearly
- Show credible leadership categories without becoming a directory
- Push users toward submitting a business problem

## Recommended Sitemap

### 1. Home
Primary conversion page. Tells the full story and drives users to submit a business problem.

### 2. How Matching Works
Deeper explanation of intake, scoping, vetting, matching, and engagement flow.

### 3. Leadership Areas
Overview page for CFO, CMO, CHRO, and CTO support, framed around business outcomes.

### 4. For Founders / Companies
Explains who Fractionalty is for, when to use it, and what problems it can solve.

### 5. For CXOs
Explains the value proposition for senior leaders who may want to join the platform.

### 6. About
Founder credibility, platform philosophy, and why Fractionalty is being built now.

### 7. Case Studies / Insights
Later-stage page for proof, case studies, thought pieces, and category education.

### 8. Contact / Intake
Primary conversion destination tied to "Tell us your business problem."

## Home Page Wireframe

### Section 1: Hero
**Goal**

Deliver the main promise fast. The visitor should immediately understand that this is a problem-led embedded leadership platform.

**Suggested copy**

**Headline**

Embedded Business Leadership, Without the Full-Time Cost

**Subheadline**

Hire vetted fractional CXOs who diagnose the real problem, lead execution, and strengthen your business without the cost or rigidity of a full-time executive.

**Primary CTA**

Tell us your business problem

**Secondary CTA**

See How Matching Works

**Optional support line**

For founders and business leaders who need real leadership, not generic advice or random talent.

**Visual direction**

Show visual tension resolving from "messy business problem" to "clear leadership match." Avoid stock handshake imagery. The visual should imply diagnosis, clarity, and movement.

### Section 2: The Problem
**Goal**

Call out the bad options founders currently face and make the status quo feel broken.

**Section heading**

Most Businesses Are Forced Into the Wrong Leadership Choice

**Intro copy**

When a business hits a leadership gap, the usual options all come with tradeoffs.

**Three-column problem framing**

**Cheap talent**

Looks affordable. Rarely owns outcomes.

**Expensive consultants**

Smart advice. Little execution ownership.

**Full-time CXO hires**

Powerful, but often too early, too slow, and too expensive.

**Closing line**

Fractionalty exists for the space in between: when you need serious leadership, but not the burden of a full-time executive hire.

### Section 3: The Fractionalty Alternative
**Goal**

Position Fractionalty as the clear middle path.

**Section heading**

Not Freelancers. Not Consultants. Embedded Leaders.

**Body copy**

Fractionalty matches businesses with vetted fractional CXOs who step into the problem, bring strategic judgment, and help drive execution where it matters most.

**Three proof blocks**

**Problem-led matching**

We start with the business problem, not a job title.

**Vetted leadership**

Every match is built around credibility, context, and fit.

**Embedded execution**

The right leader does not just advise. They help move the business forward.

### Section 4: How It Works
**Goal**

Make the process feel intelligent, high-trust, and easy to understand.

**Section heading**

From Business Problem to Embedded Leadership

**Step 1**

Tell us what is not working.

Share the business challenge in plain language, even if you are not yet sure what role you need.

**Step 2**

We scope the leadership gap.

We help translate the problem into the kind of leadership mandate your business actually needs.

**Step 3**

We match the right CXO.

We identify a vetted leader with the right functional depth, operating context, and engagement fit.

**Step 4**

Execution begins.

The engagement starts with clear ownership, defined expectations, and a leader who can move the problem forward.

**Optional note**

AI-assisted intake and matching can be referenced here as a support layer, not the centerpiece.

Suggested line:

Our intake and matching process can be AI-assisted, but every engagement is grounded in human judgment, trust, and business context.

### Section 5: Leadership Areas
**Goal**

Show breadth without becoming a directory.

**Section heading**

Leadership Areas We Help You Access

**Intro copy**

Fractionalty begins with leadership needs that are common, urgent, and hard to hire for full-time.

Fundraising readiness, financial discipline, forecasting, governance, and investor confidence.

**CMO**

Growth strategy, go-to-market clarity, brand direction, and demand creation.

**CHRO**

**CTO**

**Support line**

Need help figuring out which leadership function fits the problem? Start with the problem. We will help scope the role.

**Goal**

Answer the skeptical founder's question: why should I trust this process?

**Section heading**

Built for High-Stakes Leadership Decisions

**Proof themes**

**Vetted talent**

Credibility matters when the role influences business direction, investor confidence, people decisions, or execution speed.

**Structured matching**

We do not throw profiles at you. We work backward from the business problem.

**Outcome orientation**

The goal is not access for its own sake. The goal is progress on the problem that led you here.

**Optional slots for later**

- Case studies
- Matching speed metrics

**Goal**

Reduce friction for buyers who know they need help but are unsure what to ask for.

Many founders know something is breaking before they know what kind of leader they need. That is normal. Fractionalty is designed to help make that clearer.

Bring the business problem. We will help define the leadership need.

## Messaging Rules
- Lead with business problems, not features
- Use "embedded leadership" more than "fractional talent"
- Avoid sounding discount-led
- Avoid "AI startup" language in the hero
- Avoid jargon like "unlock synergies" or "transform your organization"
- Keep the tone calm, intelligent, and operationally credible

## Phrases To Prefer
- Embedded business leadership
- Vetted fractional CXOs
- Business problem
- Leadership gap
- Outcome ownership
- Full-time cost or rigidity
- Structured matching

## Phrases To Avoid
- Hire top talent instantly
- On-demand experts
- Marketplace of professionals
- Affordable executive talent
- AI-powered talent revolution
- End-to-end business transformation
